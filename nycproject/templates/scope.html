Scope

For this 2025 analysis, we used the provided dataset from NYC OPEN DATA.

The current dashboard uses 2025 crash records and breaks down car accidents within the boroughs of Manhattan, Queens, Bronx, Brooklyn, and Staten Island.

Some of the many questions we set out to answer:

  • Are the Highways More Dangerous than local roadways?
  • What are the more frequent contributing factors behind some of the crashes?
  • Are the Ubers really that dangerous? (j/k no specific data to draw conclusion)
  • Which borough are there a higher number of injuries/fatalities?
